# Photography provenance and reuse

This directory records research and source attribution. It does not grant rights in brand artwork or trademarks.

- Original Alcooltest review decisions and research notes follow `data/LICENSE` (CC0).
- Photographs retain the exact license recorded per file in `data/assets.json`. No third-party photograph is covered by the repository's GPL source-code license or by the original-data CC0 waiver.
- Open Food Facts image contributors license photographs under **CC BY-SA 3.0**, as linked by the [official license documentation](https://openfoodfacts.github.io/documentation/docs/Product-Opener/api/tutorials/license-be-on-the-legal-side/). Attribute the named uploader, Open Food Facts, source product page and license. Downloaded files are unmodified source images or provider-generated thumbnails; source artwork and trademarks retain any applicable rights.
- Source-derived Open Food Facts metadata, including barcodes, uploader identifiers and image identifiers in this research collection and `data/assets.json`, remains available under the [Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) and [Database Contents License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/). The original-data CC0 waiver does not replace those source terms. The source and derived metadata are distributed here for reproducibility under those terms.
- Commons photographs carry individual attribution and license URLs. Selection of one of several offered licenses is recorded per asset. Commons search metadata is evidence for review, not a guarantee that the photograph, subject or label match is correct.

Open Food Facts images are retrieved from its [documented AWS dataset](https://openfoodfacts.github.io/openfoodfacts-server/api/aws-images-dataset/). Its selected image tags are not a reliable front/rear classification: an ingredients photo can be a front label, a carton or an unrelated product. Alcooltest checks actual image contents before publication. Provider content remains untrusted.

Historical bottles, labels from different markets, partially consumed bottles, close-ups and shop photographs are explicitly described. A `pair_key` identifies a reviewed common source barcode or photographic series. It does not establish that separate uploads depict the same physical bottle. No photograph is represented as an original three-dimensional scan.
