# Photograph attribution — Volume 05

The atlas hosts **85 individually licensed reference photographs**. They cover fronts for 44 canonical products and rears for 22; 22 products have reviewed local source pairs. All 55 products have actual front and rear source evidence when the separately verified external references are included. This is not 55 locally hosted pairs.

These are genuine source photographs, including historical bottles, importer labels, close-ups and contextual scenes. The visible market and packaging may differ from a current bottling. A `pair_key` links a reviewed barcode record or photographic series; it does not prove that separate uploads show one physical bottle. A carton or partial label detail is not counted as a bottle rear.

## Rights travel with each file

- Each image below retains its own photographer credit, source URL, license or documented public-domain status, modification note and SHA-256. The GPL source-code license and original-data CC0 dedication do not relicense third-party photographs, label artwork or trademarks.
- Open Food Facts photographs retain **CC BY-SA 3.0** and contributor attribution. Source-derived photographic metadata retains **ODbL 1.0 / DbCL 1.0** as explained in the [photography license record](../data/photography/LICENSE.md) and [data license](../data/LICENSE). A source database license is different from an individual image license.
- Commons public-domain records point to the actual source rights statement where no separate license URL was supplied. They are not relabeled as CC0.
- CSS framing and illustrative 3D projections are presentation choices. The procedural bottle and projected label are artistic interpretations, not a scan, a verified complete label reconstruction or evidence of an unseen rear.
- A redistribution, adaptation or Memento image bundle must retain the attribution and exact applicable terms. Share-alike obligations remain attached to the relevant licensed image/adaptation.

The [asset manifest](../data/assets.json) is the machine-readable authority for download URLs, source image IDs, bytes, dates, license evidence and review notes. [Integrity and coverage](../data/photography/verification.json), [provider policy](../data/photography/provider-policy.json) and [photography operations](../data/photography/README.md) describe the review and bounded restore process.

## External references preserve the publisher’s copy

There are **51 verified external source records**: 11 fronts and 40 rears. Seven rear records also document useful alternative references for products that subsequently acquired a licensed local rear. These source cards link to the publisher’s page. No redistribution permission has been established for these images, and their image bytes are not hosted or shipped in the public Memento ZIP.

The [external-reference register](../data/photography/external-references.json) records publisher, source page, image URL, verified side, edition/market note, review date, review hash and rights condition. Verification concerns the photographed identity and side; it is not a license or permission grant. Eleven products still lack a local front file and 33 lack a local rear file.

## Source Memento and private research

[Source Memento](MEMENTO.md) can bundle the already hosted images with a media manifest containing credit, source, license, edition notes, byte count and local hash. It never obtains image rights by downloading an external source link. Public capsules preserve extracted facts and scoped provenance; full copyrighted articles, retailer pages, catalogue PDFs and ignored review caches are not mirrored.

## Verify the pinned collection

```sh
python3 scripts/fetch_assets.py --verify-only
```

Network restoration uses only previously accepted image URLs on the exact reviewed Commons/Open Food Facts hosts and rejects changed bytes until reviewed. See the [photographic operator guide](../data/photography/README.md).
