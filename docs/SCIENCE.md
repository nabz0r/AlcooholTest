# Evidence, mechanisms and private state transitions

Plant identity does not establish finished-beverage exposure. Distillation, maceration, dilution, volatility, dose and metabolism determine which constituents reach a drink and consumer. The curated seed has **zero finished-product laboratory measurements**. Unknown concentration never becomes zero or a guessed number.

The archive distinguishes ingredient assertions, plant–compound associations, whole-plant extract interventions, primary study metadata, mechanism assertions and measurements. Every curated assertion carries a source and limitations. Linalool odour experiments in mice, chronic oral chamomile extracts and standardized lavender oil trials cannot be re-labeled as gin effects. CB2 activity is not evidence of a THC-like high. Ethanol mechanisms are displayed separately from uncertain botanical exposure. No neural animation predicts a user's brain, intoxication or safety.

## Markov notebook

Authenticated users can record an observed transition between baseline, relaxed, sociable, sleepy and uncomfortable for a product. The API aggregates only that user's records. For states i and j, P(i,j) = N(i,j) / sum_k N(i,k). Rows without observations contain null, not artificial smoothing. The matrix therefore normalizes observed counts but does not establish the Markov property, stationarity or causality. Timing, alcohol dose, expectations, food, setting and other confounders are uncontrolled. There is no fitted pharmacokinetic model, forecast or drug-effect score.

Observations are excluded from public catalogue dumps. They remain private account data, accessible to administrators with database/admin permissions and present in private SQL backups. Avoid sensitive personal notes on a shared instance.

## Critics and monkeys

Independent reviews retain the critic's attribution and original score scale. Original short summaries avoid copying articles. Community ratings run from 0 through 5 monkeys, including a Loved it shortcut to 5. New notes await moderation. Neither system is scientific evidence. No community ratings are fabricated in seed data.
